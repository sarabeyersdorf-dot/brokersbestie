Safety check-in

Meeting someone new? Bestie checks on you.

Pick 30, 45, 60 or 90 minutes and who to text. When time is up, Bestie asks if you're OK. If you don't answer, your person gets where you are and who you're meeting.

The safety check-in is not an emergency service. If you are in danger, call 911.

Questions

Good to know

Is this a CRM?

No. Brokers Bestie is the app you use in the field: codes, tours, showings, feedback, mileage and safety. If you have Follow Up Boss, kvCORE or Lofty, connect it and your deals and appointments come in, so you never type them twice.

Do I need to download it from the App Store?

Not yet. Open brokersbestie.app on your phone and add it to your home screen. It opens like an app and works with no signal. App Store and Google Play versions are on the way.

Who can see my codes?

Only you, and anyone you invite to your team. Your data is never sold or shared for advertising, and you can download or delete all of it any time from Settings.

What happens when the free trial ends?

After 14 days you can still see everything, but you'll need a plan to add or change anything. Safety check-ins keep working either way.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time from Settings. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

How does team billing work?

One plan covers your team, priced per agent: $99 a year or $12 a month for each person on the team, on one bill.
