Privacy policy

Effective October 28, 2026

The short version

  • What you put into Brokers Bestie is yours. We use it to run the app for you and your team, and for nothing else.
  • We never sell it, and we never share it for advertising. There are no ad trackers in the app.
  • You can download everything, and you can delete your account and your data at any time from Settings.

Who we are

Brokers Bestie (“we”, “us”) is a phone app for real estate agents. Questions about this policy or your data: support@brokersbestie.app.

What we keep

  • Your account: your email address, and the profile you choose to add (name, title, phone, license numbers, brokerage, website, logo, headshot, brand colour).
  • What you add: properties, access codes, showing notes, photos and MLS sheets, tours and showing feedback, appointments and deadlines, contacts and vendors, to-dos, mileage and expenses, open house sign-ins, and safety check-ins.
  • Open house visitors: when a visitor signs in at your open house (on your phone or by scanning your QR code), the name, phone, email and answers they give are saved for you, the hosting agent.
  • Location, only when you use it: to show the property you're standing at, work out a route or a “leave now” reminder, log a drive, or attach a map to a safety alert. Bestie does not track you in the background.
  • Your CRM connection: if you connect a CRM, we keep the key or link you give us on our servers (it is never sent back to a phone) and bring in the deals, people, dates, appointments and, if you choose, contacts.
  • Reminders: if you turn on pop-up reminders, your phone's notification address and, while reminders are on, your last known location for “leave now” times.
  • Daily counts: how many times a day you use the reading and voice features, so we can apply the daily limit.

How it is used

  • To show it to you, and to your team: anything you add on a team is shared with everyone on that team.
  • To sync with the CRM you connect. Bestie only sends back what the app describes on screen (for example a deadline you tick off), and only to a CRM that accepts it.
  • To send the sign-in codes, reminders and safety texts you ask for.
  • To read an MLS sheet, a showing confirmation or something you say to Tell Bestie, when you use those features.

Texts and emails that Bestie writes for you (feedback, showing requests, recaps, seller updates, thank-yous) are sent by you, from your own phone's messaging or email app. Bestie does not send them.

Companies that help run the app

These services process data only to provide their part of Brokers Bestie:

  • Supabase: the database, file storage and sign-in.
  • Vercel: hosting the app.
  • Anthropic: reading MLS sheets, showing confirmations and spoken requests, only when you use those features. Only what you send for that request goes to it.
  • Resend: sending sign-in codes by email.
  • Twilio: sending safety check-in texts to the contact you choose.
  • Apple and Google: delivering pop-up reminders to your phone.
  • Dropbox: only the files you pick from your own Dropbox; we do not keep access to your Dropbox.
  • Your CRM (for example Follow Up Boss, kvCORE or Lofty), when you connect it.

Other people's information

Agents add information about their clients, other agents and visitors. We hold it on the agent's behalf and use it only to provide the app to that agent and their team. If you are a client or visitor and want your information changed or removed, please ask the agent you dealt with, or write to us and we will pass it on.

Keeping it safe

  • Each team's data is walled off from every other team's in the database.
  • Code links you share stop working after 24 hours, or sooner if you stop them. Open house sign-in links end within 3 days.
  • Data travels encrypted between your phone and our servers.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your data while your account is open. Settings → Delete my account deletes your sign-in and, if you are the only person on your team, everything on the team, including photos and files. If you share a team, what you shared stays with your teammates. Deleted data can stay in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before it is gone for good. Settings → Download everything gives you a copy of your data at any time.

Your rights (including California)

You can ask to know what we hold about you, to get a copy, to correct it, or to delete it. We do not sell or share personal information as California law defines those words, and we will not treat you differently for using these rights. Write to support@brokersbestie.app; we answer within 45 days.

Children

Brokers Bestie is for working real estate professionals and is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect.
