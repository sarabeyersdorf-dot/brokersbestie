Delete your account

How to delete your Brokers Bestie account and your data

In the app

  1. Open Brokers Bestie and go to Settings (the gear at the top right of Today).
  2. Scroll to the last card, Delete my account.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Delete my account.

It happens right away. You can first save a copy with Settings → Download everything.

Without the app

Email support@brokersbestie.app from the email address you sign in with, with the subject Delete my account. We confirm by email and delete it, usually within a few days and always within 45 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in and your profile (name, license numbers, logo, headshot).
  • If you are the only person on your team: everything on the team, including properties, codes, notes, tours, contacts, vendors, mileage, photos, MLS sheets, open house sign-ins and your CRM connection.
  • If you share a team: your sign-in and profile go; what you shared stays with your teammates.

Deleted data can stay in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before it is gone for good. If you paid for a plan, Stripe, our payment processor, keeps its own payment records as the law requires.
